Visual Lessons

Simple diagrams to help build intuition before memorizing details.

PROPAGATION

Skywave path

Some HF signals can return toward Earth after interacting with ionized regions of the upper atmosphere. Conditions vary with frequency, time, season, and solar activity.

ELECTRONICS

Simple series circuit

In a simple series circuit, the same current flows through each component. Voltage is shared across components according to their resistance and the circuit conditions.

ANTENNAS

Half-wave dipole

A resonant half-wave dipole has two arms, each roughly a quarter wavelength long. Practical length depends on frequency, construction, and surroundings.

SIGNAL BASICS

Bandwidth and selectivity

Bandwidth describes the range of frequencies passed by a filter or occupied by a signal. A narrower receiver filter can reduce nearby interference, but if it is too narrow for the signal, it may distort the audio or signal information.
